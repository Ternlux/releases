# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately, not in a public issue:

- Use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on the Security tab of this
  repository, or
- Email **support@ternlux.com** with "SECURITY" in the subject.

Include the version, your OS, steps to reproduce, and the impact you see. We aim
to acknowledge reports within 3 business days.

## Scope

In scope: this repository's code, including the Desktop App, the bundled
plugins, and official builds from
[github.com/Ternlux/releases](https://github.com/Ternlux/releases).

You may review, test and modify this code on your own systems. Don't test
against Ternlux-operated servers (for example `api.ternlux.com`) without written
permission; see the Ternlux Acceptable Use Policy.

## Disclosure

Please give us a reasonable chance to fix the issue before disclosing it
publicly, and no later than 90 days after your report. We won't take legal
action against good-faith research that follows this policy and the safe-harbor
terms in the Acceptable Use Policy.
