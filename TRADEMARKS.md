## What you can do without asking

- Say truthfully that your software is based on, works with, or is a fork of
  Ternlux ("Built on Ternlux", "a fork of Ternlux").
- Use the name to refer to the official project in articles, talks, tutorials
  and reviews.
- Build and run unmodified source for your own use under the Ternlux name.

## What needs our written permission

- Distributing a modified build under the name "Ternlux" or with the Ternlux
  logo or icon. Rename your fork and replace the icon in `assets/`.
- Using the name or logo in a product, company, or domain name, or in a way that
  suggests Ternlux Inc. endorses or provides your product.
- Changing `appId` (`com.ternlux.app`) to something else is fine; keeping it in
  a public fork is not, because it collides with the official app's data and
  updates.

Official builds are published only at
[github.com/Ternlux/releases](https://github.com/Ternlux/releases) and
[ternlux.com](https://www.ternlux.com/).
